# Too many buses? No: the delay follows the clock

30 October 2026. Tests of H3 and H4 from the [hypotheses](hypotheses-2026-10-04.md),
both of which blamed slow hops on buses getting in each other's way.

**Result: both refuted.** On the same roads, an hour with as many buses as the rush
hour but outside it loses almost nothing. The delay belongs to the time of day,
which means general traffic, not the buses themselves. This fits the 2 October finding
that lateness belongs to [roads at given hours](roads-not-vehicles-2026-10-02.md).

Data: autumn Monday to Thursday working days, 2 September to 29 October 2026 (34 days),
29 October from 20:00 local dropped ([collection gap](collection-gap-2026-10-29.md)).
"Buses per hour" is hops observed on the link per day in that hour. For the Oslo st.
links it matches the timetable of 7 October to within 2 an hour (22, 21, 18 at 06, 07, 08).

---

## H3. Lazdynėliai's morning is buses queuing at Oslo st.

*Pre-registered test:* hour by hour, does delay on the exit links follow the number of
buses, or the time of day regardless of bus count? *Supported if* it follows bus count.

The three exit links from the Oslo st. stops (to Erfurto st. twice, to Jonažolių st.):

| hour | 06 | 07 | 08 | 09 | 13 | 16 | 17 | 18 | 21 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| buses per hour | 22.7 | 20.4 | 19.5 | 18.8 | 19.6 | 19.9 | 18.6 | 21.1 | 17.7 |
| seconds lost per hop | −0.5 | **+47.7** | **+49.0** | +1.7 | −0.6 | +14.4 | **+47.3** | +0.8 | −9.6 |

- The busiest hour is 06, and it loses nothing. 18 carries more buses than 07 or 08
  and loses under a second.
- 07 to 08 against 06 and 18: **+48.2 s per hop, 95% range +41.5 to +55.4.**
- With the hour held fixed, one more bus an hour changes the delay by **−0.18 s
  (95% range −1.16 to +0.74)**: nothing.
- The evening peak at 17 is as bad as the morning one, with fewer buses.
- Each of the three links shows the same pattern on its own.

**Verdict: refuted.** Buses at Oslo st. are not queuing behind each other; they are
stuck in the 07 to 09 and 17 traffic like everyone else. A second stopping bay would
not recover the half minute. Something that takes buses out of general traffic on
the way to Laisvės pr. could. The summer to autumn jump (+2 s to +48 s at 07 to 08)
coincides with school starting, which is what [H8](h8-groundwork-2026-10-18.md) tests.

## H4. The evening core slowdown is too many buses per stop

*Pre-registered test:* delay against buses per hour per central stop pair, with the
hour held fixed. *Supported if* delay rises steeply above some number of buses an hour.

Hops starting 0.5 to 2 km from Cathedral Square, seconds lost per hop by how many buses
use the link in that hour:

| buses per hour | 0-4 | 4-8 | 8-12 | 12-20 | 20-30 | 30-45 | 45+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| evening 16-18 | +29.7 | +33.7 | +24.5 | +19.7 | **+9.0** | +12.4 | +37.6 |
| midday 11-13 | +5.2 | +8.6 | +1.9 | +1.5 | +3.4 | +3.4 | +4.7 |

At a fixed hour, links with 20 to 45 buses an hour lose **less** than quiet ones.
The 45+ group is 8 links, and they show what is going on:

| link | 06: buses, s | 13: buses, s | 17: buses, s |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kalvarijų turgus → Žaliasis tiltas | 50, −11 | 31, −5 | 47, **+126** |
| Rūdninkų st. → MO muziejus | 40, −6 | 49, +5 | 62, **+82** |
| Kalnų parkas → Šv. Petro ir Povilo bažnyčia | 24, +4 | 38, +2 | 53, +30 |
| MO muziejus → Reformatų st. | 30, 0 | 36, −7 | 42, +23 |
| Vinco Kudirkos aikštė → Islandijos st. | 46, +1 | 40, +6 | 46, +7 |
| Islandijos st. → Vinco Kudirkos aikštė | 31, +3 | 37, +12 | 45, +9 |
| Tuskulėnų rimties parkas → Minties st. | 33, −7 | 30, −8 | 35, −6 |

Kalvarijų turgus to Žaliasis tiltas carries 50 buses at 06 and loses nothing, then
47 at 17 and loses two minutes. Four of the busiest links barely lose time at any hour.
Within a link, with link and hour held fixed, one more bus an hour changes the
delay by **−0.18 s** (3,274 link-hours).

**Verdict: refuted.** There is no level of buses per hour above which delay climbs.
The evening core is slow on particular roads at particular hours, mostly the bridge
approaches (Žaliasis tiltas, Rūdninkų st. to MO muziejus), whatever the bus count.

*A note on the basis.* H4 was justified by the evening slowdown being symmetric in
both directions. The [one direction](one-direction-2026-10-02.md) study found it is
not quite: within 2 km, evening inbound hops lose +40.3 s and outbound +7.9 s. The
test above does not depend on that.

## Limits

- The test is hourly, as registered. Two buses arriving at a stop within a minute of
  each other could still delay each other; that needs positions, which only the server holds.
- Bus counts are observed hops. Across days within a link and hour, a busier day is
  very slightly slower in the evening (+0.8 s per extra bus, 95% range +0.4 to +1.1),
  and not at midday. Ten extra buses an hour would add 8 s. It is too small to
  explain the evening.

## Method

`code/bus_count_tests.py`, from `analysis/trav-*.csv.*` and stop positions in
`gtfs/gtfs-*.zip`. Cathedral Square at 54.68566, 25.28767. Intervals are a bootstrap
over days. Runs in about a minute from a fresh clone.
