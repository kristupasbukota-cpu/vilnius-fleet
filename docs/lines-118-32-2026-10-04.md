# Bus lines 118 and 32: a closer look

4 October 2026. A detailed look at two bus lines that both end at Skalvių st. in
Perkūnkiemis: 118 from Pilaitė (Bitėnų st.) and 32 from Žvėrynas (Žvėryno žiedas).
Made for exploring the data; it tests no hypothesis. Working days, summer 18 to 31
August against autumn 2 September to 2 October. Bus only: trolleybus lines with the
same numbers are left out.

**In short**

- **118 is a small, stable line; 32 is not.** 118 ran on 8 vehicles in autumn, and three
  of them ran 91% of its trips. 32 drew on 43 vehicles, and its three busiest ran 15%.
- **Autumn added one sharp hour to both lines, in both directions.** Between 07:00 and
  07:59, 118 loses +23 s per hop towards Perkūnkiemis and +18 s the other way, against
  −12 and −1 in August. 32: +16 and +8, against 0 and −4. By 08:00 it has mostly gone.
  Unlike the network's morning step, it is not only inbound. It fits the school-run
  hypothesis (H8), not yet tested.
- **118's 07:00 trips into Perkūnkiemis end a median 12 minutes late** (30 trips),
  against +36 s over the whole day. Most of the morning loss is near the end, from
  Antežeriai onwards: +58 and +53 s per hop on the two links to Naujosios Gineitiškės
  between 07:00 and 09:00, and +135 s on the next one, though that is measured on only
  28 trips.
- **32's timetable puts 5 minutes on one link and too little on its neighbours.** Ąžuolų
  st. to Sodų st. is scheduled at 5:00 and takes 2:24 towards Perkūnkiemis; the link
  before it, Buivydiškės to Ąžuolų st., is scheduled at 2:00 and takes 3:14. Per hop,
  the first looks like the worst link on the line and the second like the best, while
  over the pair buses run 83 s early. The same happens the other way (−46 and −33 s
  over the two pairs). This is the pattern behind hypothesis H7, *spare time in the
  wrong places*, on one line.
- **What that does to punctuality on 32.** Halfway along, just before the padded link,
  45% of buses towards Perkūnkiemis are more than 3 minutes late. A quarter of the way
  further on, 21% are more than a minute early. In the other direction, 28% are early
  at the halfway point. At the far end, 68% and 63% of 32's trips are on time, against
  84% and 75% for 118.

## A caution for the link rankings

Rankings of "the links that lose the most time" measure each hop against the time the
timetable gives that hop. Where the timetable gives one link too much and the next too
little, the second ranks as slow although nothing slows buses there. On 32, the line's
"worst link" is an artefact of this kind. Earlier network-wide link rankings
([roads, not vehicles](roads-not-vehicles-2026-10-02.md),
[bus lanes](bus-lanes-2026-10-02.md)) have not yet been checked for such pairs. That
check belongs with H7.

## Method and files

- `code/lines_118_32.py`, run from the repository root, reproduces every number here.
  It reads `analysis/trav-*`, `analysis/blocks-*.json.gz` and `analysis/spacing-*.csv.xz`,
  and needs pandas. Stop ids in its output map to names through the timetables in
  `gtfs/`.
- The interactive page with maps and charts of these two lines is a private exploration
  page and is not part of the repository.
