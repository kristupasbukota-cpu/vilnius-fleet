# Bus 63: the lost minutes sit at the far end, every day since August

30 October 2026. A first look, not a finished result. Follows up the
[spare time](spare-time-2026-10-06.md) note that bus 63 runs 2 to 5 minutes over its
timetable at every hour.

**Result: two hops at the Pilaitė end carry almost all of it, and the timetable's last
outbound stops are almost never recorded.** Confirming the road the buses take needs
vehicle positions, which only the server holds.

Data: Monday to Thursday, 2 September to 29 October 2026, `analysis/trav-*`, and the
timetable of 2 October.

| hop | direction | hops | scheduled | lost per hop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kriaučiūnai → Papiškės (19051 → 7700) | outbound | 846 | 248 s | **+220 s** |
| Papiškės → Vaivadiškės (7700 → 7702) | outbound | **13** | 120 s | +22 s |
| Vaivadiškės → Papiškės (7703 → 7701) | inbound | 808 | 154 s | **+369 s** |
| Kriaučiūnai → Padekaniškės (7699 → 8371) | inbound | 859 | 120 s | +69 s |
| every other hop | both | | | under +20 s |

- **Outbound trips stop being recorded at Papiškės.** The timetable continues to
  Vaivadiškės (7702) and a terminus (7692). Only 13 of 846 trips are seen on that hop.
- **Inbound trips are first recorded at Vaivadiškės (7703)**, not at the timetabled first
  stop, Karveliškių kapinės (4686). Their first hop takes about 8.7 minutes for about
  1.2 km.
- **It is the same every day from 18 August**, the first day of data. Weekday inbound
  runs +300 to +550 s. Weekends are lower. Outbound eased from about +300 s in
  September to about +150 s in October. So this is not a short roadworks diversion
  inside the record.
- **The operator's own feed agrees that 63 runs late, but by less.** Its delay field puts
  the median trip at about 3.4 minutes late when first seen and 3.6 (outbound) to 4.6
  (inbound) minutes late when last seen (`analysis/blocks-*`, weekdays 20 to 29
  October). The inbound trip gains about a minute end to end, not the 6 minutes its
  first hop shows. So most of the +369 s is probably time standing at the far end,
  not time on the road.

**Reading:** buses on 63 most likely turn round or stand somewhere other than the
timetable shows, around Papiškės and Vaivadiškės, and the time this takes is not in the
timetable. They then start the next trip about 3.4 minutes late. That fits "late at
every hour" better than a road diversion. No 2026 notice of a 63 route change was
found on JUDU's site.

**To settle it:** the raw positions of 63 buses between Papiškės and Karveliškės on one
weekday, from the server (read only).

**Note for 1 to 2 November:** JUDU usually adds 63 service to Karveliškės cemetery for
Vėlinės. Those days are outside every pre-registered test.
